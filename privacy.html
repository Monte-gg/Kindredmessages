About Kindred

Kindred is a small, independent messaging app for chatting with friends and family. It is a personal project and is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft, Apple, Meta, any bank, or any other company.

What Kindred never asks for

Kindred never asks for your password to another service, bank or card details, phone verification codes, or for you to download or install software. Your Kindred password is only for Kindred, and you should use a password that you don't use anywhere else.

What we store

  • The email address, username, and optional profile picture you sign up with.
  • The messages and files you send, so they can sync between your devices.
  • Basic sign-in activity, such as when you logged in, to help keep accounts safe.

Accounts and data are handled by Supabase. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored in plain text. Your email address is never shown to other members.

Your choices

You can stop using Kindred at any time. To have your account and its data deleted, contact the site owner through the project's GitHub page.
